What Talent Finder is

Talent Finder is a public directory of skilled hands in Ghana and Zambia. A home, a factory, or a company searches a trade, opens a Talent ID, and looks at photos of finished work.

How it works

A skilled person posts a name, a skill, a town, and photos of their own work. They receive a Talent ID. A hirer searches that skill, agrees to check the person, and only then sees the messaging IDs the talent chose to show.

Who it is for

It is for households, factories, and companies that need a plumber, electrician, carpenter, welder, painter, gardener, tailor, cleaner, cook, or home tutor nearby. It is also for the person who does that work and wants to be found.

Questions

Does Talent Finder check the person?

No. It shows the work the person posted. You must check the skill yourself before you hire.

Which countries are listed?

Ghana and Zambia.

What is public on a Talent ID?

The name, skill, town, years, a description, photos of the work, and the messaging IDs the person turns on. An email address, a phone number, and an identity document are not shown.
